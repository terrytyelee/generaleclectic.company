# General Eclectic — Brand Guidelines

**G.E. Creative Infrastructures**
Version 1.0 — April 2026

## 1. Brand Identity

General Eclectic is a creative infrastructure company building intelligence tools for artistry. The brand sits at the intersection of institutional gravitas and creative energy — a holding company that takes music, culture, and technology as seriously as finance takes capital.

The name "General Eclectic" is a deliberate play on General Electric — borrowing the weight of American industrial infrastructure and redirecting it toward creative output. We build the pipes, the rails, the power grid — but for artists.

**Tagline:** *Intelligence for Artistry.*

**Parent entity:** G.E. Creative Infrastructures

---

## 2. Mission & Values

**Mission:** To build the creative infrastructure that makes artistry more intelligent and intelligence more artistic.

**Values:**

- **Rigor meets taste.** Every product, every report, every interface reflects both analytical depth and aesthetic sensitivity.
- **Infrastructure over spectacle.** We build lasting systems, not hype cycles.
- **Artistry is serious work.** We treat creative output with the same precision as quantitative analysis.
- **Eclecticism is a feature.** We draw from music, neuroscience, data science, and design — because the best ideas live at intersections.

## 3. Logo & Wordmark

The General Eclectic wordmark is set in **IBM Plex Sans ExtraBold (800 weight)**, all uppercase. This is the primary brand identifier.

### Primary Wordmark
- **GENERAL ECLECTIC** — stacked, two lines
- Set in IBM Plex Sans, weight 800
- Letter-spacing: -0.04em (tight tracking)
- Line-height: 0.9 (very tight leading)
- Always uppercase

### Monogram
- **GE** — used as watermark, favicon, and shorthand
- Same typeface and weight as wordmark
- Used at very low opacity (4%) as background watermark on brand surfaces

### Eyebrow
- **G.E. Creative Infrastructures** — appears above the wordmark
- IBM Plex Sans, weight 600
- Font size: ~0.7rem relative to context
- Letter-spacing: 0.18em
- All uppercase
- Always in Golden Yellow (#F0B429)

### Primary Colors

|-------|------|-----|-----|-------|
| ██ | **English Green** | `#1B4332` | 27, 67, 50 | Primary background, brand anchor |
| ██ | **Golden Yellow** | `#F0B429` | 240, 180, 41 | Accents, eyebrows, taglines, CTAs |
| ██ | **White** | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Primary text on dark backgrounds |
| ██ | **Black** | `#0A0A0A` | 10, 10, 10 | Text on light backgrounds, print |

### Extended Palette

| Color | Name | Hex | Usage |
|-------|------|-----|-------|
| ██ | Signal Green | `#34D399` | Active status indicators |
| ██ | Deep Forest | `#143328` | Darker tones, hover states |
| ██ | Light Gold | `#F5CC5E` | Highlights, secondary accents |
| ██ | Warm Gray | `#9CA3AF` | Muted text, metadata |

### Color Usage Rules

- English Green is the dominant brand color. It should occupy at least 70% of any brand surface.
- Golden Yellow is used sparingly for emphasis — eyebrows, taglines, interactive elements. Never as a background fill.
- White text on English Green is the default type treatment.
- The GE watermark uses white at 4% opacity on English Green backgrounds.

### Light Mode — White Backgrounds

When working on white or light backgrounds (print, co-branded materials, documents, light-UI contexts), the following inversions apply:

| Element | Dark Mode (default) | Light Mode (white bg) |
|---------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| Background | English Green #1B4332 | White #FFFFFF |
| Wordmark | White #FFFFFF | English Green #1B4332 |
| Eyebrow | Golden Yellow #F0B429 | English Green #1B4332 (50% opacity) |
| Tagline | Golden Yellow #F0B429 | English Green #1B4332 |
| Body text | White (60% opacity) | Black #0A0A0A |
| Labels/meta | White (25% opacity) | Warm Gray #9CA3AF |
| Dividers | White (8% opacity) | Black (8% opacity) |
| GE Watermark | White (4% opacity) | English Green (4% opacity) |
| Status: Active | Signal Green #34D399 | Signal Green #34D399 |
| Status: Product | Golden Yellow #F0B429 | Golden Yellow #F0B429 (darkened to #D49B1F on white) |

**Key principles for light mode:**

- The wordmark is ALWAYS English Green on white — never black, never gray.
- Golden Yellow does not have enough contrast on white for body text. Use it only for small accents or badges with a tinted background (e.g., gold text on 8% gold fill).
- The tagline switches from gold to English Green in italic serif. The italic serif already carries enough visual distinction without needing the color shift.
- The GE watermark remains present but uses English Green at 4% opacity instead of white.
- English Green should still feel dominant — even on white backgrounds, it's the primary text and accent color. The brand should never feel "light and airy." It should feel like a serious document happened to be printed on white paper.

**When to use light mode:**

- Print documents, contracts, and legal materials
- Co-branded decks where a partner requires white backgrounds
- Email templates and newsletters
- Light-mode app contexts where the system enforces a white canvas
- Formal reports and investor-facing documents

**When to stay dark:**

- Website and digital product surfaces (default)
- Decks and presentations (default)
- Social media and marketing materials
- Event materials and signage
- Brand guidelines and internal creative

## 5. Typography

### Type Family: IBM Plex

General Eclectic uses the full IBM Plex family — Sans, Serif, and Mono — to express the brand's range from institutional authority to creative nuance.

#### IBM Plex Sans
- **Role:** Headlines, UI, navigation, body text
- **Weights used:** 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 600 (SemiBold), 700 (Bold), 800 (ExtraBold)
- **Headlines:** ExtraBold (800), tight tracking (-0.04em), uppercase
- **Body:** Regular (400) or Medium (500)
- **Labels/metadata:** SemiBold (600), wide tracking (0.12em), uppercase, small size

#### IBM Plex Serif
- **Role:** Taglines, editorial accent, pull quotes
- **Weights used:** 400 Regular, 400 Italic
- **Always italic when used as a tagline** (e.g., *Intelligence for Artistry.*)
- Provides warmth and editorial credibility against the Sans-heavy system

#### IBM Plex Mono
- **Role:** Technical labels, product names, code references, tool badges
- **Weights used:** 400, 500, 600
- Used for product identifiers (e.g., `artiste.md`, `signal`)
- Wide tracking (0.06em) for small labels

### Type Hierarchy

| Level | Typeface | Weight | Size | Tracking | Case |
|-------|----------|--------|------|----------|------|
| Display | Plex Sans | 800 | clamp(4.5rem, 13vw, 11rem) | -0.04em | UPPER |
| Eyebrow | Plex Sans | 600 | 0.7rem | 0.18em | UPPER |
| Tagline | Plex Serif | 400 Italic | clamp(1.2rem, 2.5vw, 1.7rem) | default | Sentence |
| Section head | Plex Sans | 700 | 1.3rem | -0.02em | Sentence |
| Body | Plex Sans | 400 | 0.88rem | default | Sentence |
| Label | Plex Sans | 500–600 | 0.55–0.65rem | 0.08–0.12em | UPPER |
| Code/Tool | Plex Mono | 500 | 0.65rem | 0.06em | lower |

## 7. Imagery & Tone

### Visual Tone
- **Institutional but not corporate.** Think Bloomberg Terminal meets Criterion Collection.
- **Dark, grounded, confident.** The brand lives in dark mode.
- **Restrained luxury.** Gold accents feel earned, not decorative.
- **Typographic-first.** Words and type are the primary visual medium. Photography is secondary.

### Photography (when used)
- Moody, high-contrast, cinematic grain
- Never stock-photo energy — always feel candid or editorial
- Prefer black and white or desaturated with selective color
- Artists, studios, instruments, screens — never posed corporate shots

### Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Use generous negative space | Clutter layouts with elements |
| Let type dominate the visual hierarchy | Rely on imagery to carry the brand |
| Keep gold accents surgical | Use gold as a primary fill color |
| Embrace darkness and restraint | Use bright backgrounds or gradients |
| Make the watermark whisper | Make the watermark prominent |

## 9. Digital Application

### Website — generaleclectic.company
- Full English Green hero, full-viewport
- Property cards below fold with thin white separators (10% opacity)
- Links styled as subtle rows, not buttons
- Low-key embedded player (45% opacity, reveals on hover)
- Minimal footer: copyright + domain in small type

### Status Indicators
- Active: Signal Green (#34D399) text on 10% green background
- Product: Golden Yellow (#F0B429) text on 10% gold background

### Interactive States
- Hover: subtle border brightening, element translation
- Transitions: 0.2–0.4s ease timing
- Never use harsh or sudden state changes
